PAPER DRIFT 纸龙夜游 — a paper-cut night flight: pilot a paper dragon through 9 waves of ink clouds, firework rockets and ink moths, collect glowing lanterns to build combos, choose 1-of-3 upgrades between waves, and slay 3 ink phoenix bosses to win. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best game" bounty, $1,000 in USDC.

Controls: WASD / arrows to fly, mouse to aim, hold Space or click to breathe fire, Shift to dash, P to pause, M to mute. Fire has recoil, kills trigger hit-stop and screen shake, and every pickup rings a pentatonic chime. Three bosses guard waves 3, 6 and 9; clear wave 9 for victory, then keep going in endless mode.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — the game is plain static files under `game/`, no build step, no network request.
